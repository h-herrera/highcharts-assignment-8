How Many People are Visiting Florida?

By: Hailey Herrera

The data above is from Visitflorida.org. The data is found under the "Top Origin Countries" subheading.

Acknowledging the approximate number of visitors traveling to Florida is important for marking purposes, as well as giving guidance to businesses on how to better their practices and inclusivity. In tourist-rich areas, businesses can use the data from the top regions that travel to Florida to create alternative advertisements or signs in a popular language of that region. Businesses can also try to hire employees who speak other languages, in addition to English, to improve inclusivity. These practices can help visitors feel more welcome and could incline them to spend their money. Applying this data to business practices will increase the reputation and impression of not only the brand but the Florida experience, too.

The data above is from Visitflorida.org. The data is found under the "Florida Visitor Estimates" subheading.

The quarterly reports of visitors show the most popular times of the year that people travel to Florida. This information lets businesses, that are affected by tourism, know to prepare for an increase or decrease in business traffic. It also helps them organize their inventory so that the appropriate amount of merchandise is available. Businesses in tourist-rich areas may choose to have their sale prices around the times with the most customer traffic. Understanding the visitor data of Florida is primarily beneficial to businesses, so opportunities and lasting memories can be created for the visitors.
